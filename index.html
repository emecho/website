Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order a service for my two or four-wheeler?

Visit the Emecho website or mobile app, fill in your vehicle details and service requirements, and submit the form. You'll receive a service number and scheduled pickup date. Our garage mechanic will collect your vehicle at your convenience.

Are emergency services available 24/7?

Yes, all services are available 24/7. However, some complex services may require vehicle pickup to our garage for proper diagnosis and repair. We'll inform you of any requirements upfront.

How do scheduled services work?

Scheduled services are periodic maintenance plans customized to your vehicle type. Select your preferred service package, pay the advance fee, and we'll notify you one week before service. Services are performed at your location or our center based on your preference.

What quality assurance do you provide?

All services include quality assurance certification. We stand behind our work with customer satisfaction guarantees. Your feedback directly impacts our service improvements and quality standards.
